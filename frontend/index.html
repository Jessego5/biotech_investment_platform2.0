About the data

Every figure is computed from a primary source and says which one. Nothing is estimated, and a missing figure is shown as missing rather than as a zero.

  • Trials — ClinicalTrials.gov. A pipeline counts only the trials a company leads; ones it collaborates on are kept and labelled apart.
  • Financials — SEC EDGAR company facts. Runway divides liquidity by cash actually used in operations, not by R&D expense, and says when it fell back.
  • Patents and exclusivity — FDA Orange Book and Purple Book. US approvals only: protection held elsewhere is not shown at all.
  • Annual report text — 10-K, 20-F and 40-F narrative, chunked and embedded for search.
